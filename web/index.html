How it works

  1. One quote a day

    A new quote about overcoming appears every day at midnight, in your own time zone. The old one drops away and the new one comes down from above.

  2. Copy

    Click or tap the quote to copy it, together with its author.

  3. Search

    Right-click the quote to search for it on the web, or right-click the author to look them up.

  4. Share

    The arrow next to this button turns the quote on screen into a square image. On a phone it opens the share sheet; on a computer it saves the picture.

  5. Past days

    Press ← and → on a keyboard, or swipe left and right on a phone, to walk back through every day since Day 1.

  6. The day counter

    The number in the corner counts the days since the first one. Open it to read every quote so far. Hover over it to see how long until the next.

  7. Night

    From 9 p.m. to 6 a.m. the page turns white on black, and back again in the morning.

  8. Offline

    Once you have visited, the site keeps working without a connection.

  9. Underneath

    Every quote, and the calendar that picks them, lives inside a WebAssembly module written by hand: hundreds of quotes, scheduled for the next four hundred years.
